# Privacy boundaries

## Public research dashboard

May contain:

- Public papers, trials, regulatory communications, and creator metadata
- User preferences and watchlists for public entities/topics
- Prototype export/import JSON for preferences

Must not contain:

- Personal lab results
- Diagnoses
- Medication or supplement dose records
- Other sensitive personal health data

## My Healthspan (future)

A separate local encrypted companion for personal tracking. Architecturally and operationally separate from Healthspan Dashboard. Do not merge personal databases into a hosted public site.
