# Methodology — Intervention identity, regulation & safety (M4 residual)

## Identity

Exact-first entity resolution is preferred. Fuzzy matches never auto-merge. Append-only resolution decisions support accept/map/create/ambiguous/reject/defer/split/redirect.

## Regulation

AU/US jurisdictions stay separate. Product-scoped assertions preserve:

- miss ≠ unapproved
- trial ≠ authorization
- label presence ≠ approval

Allowed label sections for display: warnings, contraindications, indications, boxed warnings, adverse reactions (high-level). Dosage-and-administration is excluded from dossier display.

## Safety

FDA AEMS potential signals are never proven causality. Spontaneous-report aggregates always carry:

> Spontaneous-report counts are not incidence, causality, or ranking. Zero reports ≠ safe.

TGA DAEN is not imported.

## Comparison

2–4 entity comparison has no winner, rank, recommendation, or report-count safety ranking.
